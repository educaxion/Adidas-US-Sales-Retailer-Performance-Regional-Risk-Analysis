# Adidas-US-Sales-Retailer-Performance-Regional-Risk-Analysis

## Project Overview
Adidas management wants to evaluate its US sales performance to optimize partner distribution, identify high-margin regional markets, and mitigate channel dependency risks.

### Business Question
* Which retail partners drive the highest revenue, profit, and margin efficiency?
* How do sales methods (*In-Store, Outlet, Online*) impact retailer profitability?
* Are there regional dependency risks where Adidas relies too heavily on a single retail partner?
* Which states and regions offer the highest operating margins for strategic growth?

## Key Business Findings
* **Top Retailer Partner**: **West Gear** is the top retail partner with the highest revenue generation at **$242.96M** (27% market share), followed by Foot Locker (**$220.10M**), and Sports Direct (**$182.47M**).
* **Top Margin Retailer**: **Sports Direct** achived the highest profitability efficient with a **40.74% weighted operating margin**, outperforming West Gear (**36.88%**) and Walmart (**34.66%**).
* **Top Margin State**: **Alabama** generated the highest profit efficiency among all states with an average operating margin of **55.10%** ($9.15M profit), followed closely by Tennessee (**50.25% margin**).
* **Top Revenue Region**: The **West Region** generated the highest total revenue at **$269.9M** and total profit of **$89.6M**, making it the primary volume driver for Adidas US.
* **Top Margin Region**: The South Region achieved the highest overall average operating margin at **45.12%** ($61.1M profit on $144.7M revenue), proving to be the most margin-efficient geographic market.

## Business Recommendation
* **Diversify Retailer Risk in High-Volume Regions**: In the **West region**, Adidas relies heavily on **West Gear**, which controls **50.96%** of regional sales. Adidas should expand partnership with secondary retailers (such as Foot Locker or Amazon) in the West region to mitigate dependency risk.
* **Capitalize on High-Margin States for Expansion**: States in the South region, such as **Alabama (55.10% margin)** and **Tennessee (50.25% margin)**, demonstrate exceptional profitability despite lower overall volume. Adidas should prioritize marketing investments and retail coverage expansion in these high-efficiency markets.
* **Optimize Online Sales Channel Synergies**: Online channels consistently yield higher operating margins across retail partners. Adidas should strengthen digital distribution collaborations with e-commerce-focused platforms like Amazon and Walmart to drive bottom-line profit without incurring high physical store overheads.
